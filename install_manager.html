Pay Rates
Set the dollar amount per piece-rate item for each tier (Helper 1–3, Lead 1–3) and the hourly rate per tier. When a tech logs work, the rate for their tier fills in automatically and is locked — techs pick the items and the quantity but cannot change a rate. Only management can change a rate, here or with Edit on a turn-in. Changing a rate here only affects new entries, not pay already logged.
